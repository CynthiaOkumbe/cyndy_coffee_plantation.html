Welcome to Green Hills Coffee Plantation

Green Hills Coffee Plantation is a family-owned coffee farm located in the lush hills of Colombia. We specialize in producing high-quality, sustainably grown coffee beans that are carefully harvested and roasted to perfection. Our plantation is committed to preserving the environment and supporting local communities through fair trade practices and eco-friendly farming methods.

Our Coffee

We offer a variety of coffee blends, each with its own unique flavor profile. From rich and bold dark roasts to smooth and aromatic light roasts, our coffee is sure to satisfy every palate. Our beans are sourced from the finest coffee-growing regions in Colombia, ensuring that every cup of Green Hills Coffee is a true taste of excellence.
